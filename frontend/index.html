Only key is required. Fields left out are simply not scraped for this run.

Field reference

  • key
    Unique id, e.g. acme_corp. Required.
  • display_name
    Name shown in the app.
  • linkedin_url
    Company or profile URL.
  • twitter_handle
    With or without @.
  • reddit_query · news_query
    Search terms; quote exact names.
  • sec_cik
    10-digit SEC company id.
  • blog_url · rss_url
    Insights page or feed.
  • youtube_channel_id
    The UC… id.
  • patents_query
    Inventor name (people).
